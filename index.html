Our Purpose

Born from the Intersection of Acoustics and Architecture

Founded in Copenhagen and San Francisco, AURA was created with a clear rebellion against planned obsolescence and excessive plastic tech gadgets.

Every curve, chamfered edge, and transducer we engineer is designed to withstand decades of daily devotion. We fuse authentic sustainable materials—solid aerospace titanium, American hardwoods, and recycled aerospace aluminum—with state-of-the-art acoustic algorithms.
